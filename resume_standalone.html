“AI has fundamentally transformed software engineering: traditional frontend/backend or Java/Python/PHP silos are dissolving into a single role — the AI Engineer as a Conductor.”

Since diving into AI research and real-world adoption in early 2023 and sharing ongoing reflections on my technical blog & WeChat column "ShenBiJun (神笔君)", I have witnessed a paradigm shift in how software is built. In the AI era, an engineer acts as a Conductor. As code syntax becomes increasingly automated, what truly differentiates engineers going forward is full-stack individual combat capability, team collaboration, precise requirement articulation, rigorous logical modeling, and execution.
